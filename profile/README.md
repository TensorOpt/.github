# Your users search. Do they find?

Independent, statistically sound search and RAG evaluation where a wrong result is expensive.

## The Blind Spot

Users judge what search shows them, and nobody sees what it missed.
People judge search by what appears on the screen.
What it missed never appears, so nobody complains about it.
In legal and financial work, that blind spot is expensive.

Searchers believed they had found at least 75% of the relevant documents; measured recall averaged about 20% [(Blair and Maron, 1985)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3166.3197).
The caveat: an old Boolean system used in legal discovery.
The point is the gap between belief and measurement, not the number.

Tested AI legal research tools “each hallucinate between 17% and 33% of the time” [(Magesh et al., 2025, Journal of Empirical Legal Studies)](https://isps.yale.edu/resource/hallucination-free-assessing-the-reliability-of-leading-ai-legal-research-tools).

## Who This Is For?

### Document Search & RAG (on any stack)

Independent evaluation of search, Q&A, and RAG **for high-stakes use cases**; eg., contracts, case law, regulations, filings, policies, and research.
A missed or wrong document, clause, or figure can have legal or financial consequences, and nobody notices at the time, because people only see what search returned.

### Portals & intranets (Liferay)

A free open-source plugin [liferay-search-eval-logger](https://github.com/TensorOpt/liferay-search-eval-logger) records user searches and the results shown, which the base Liferay DXP does not keep.
When you have data, TensorOpt measures it independently.
